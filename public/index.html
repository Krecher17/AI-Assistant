Desktop companion

Grim follows your pointer as a tiny resting companion. Say “Hey Grim” and he emerges to listen, work, and speak, then returns to the cursor. The dashboard opens only when you open the app or choose Open dashboard in the tray.

The companion is click-through: it never replaces the Windows pointer or blocks your clicks. Use the tray menu to hide the character or pause the microphone.

Voice

Files you choose

No folders are enabled by default. Only enable folders whose contents you want the assistant to access.

Local storage

SQLite stores chats, memory, imported text, and reminder history. It is not encrypted at rest. API keys and Gmail tokens use Windows account encryption.

CONNECTIONS

Your phone, in the loop.

A USB browser companion is available for testing: use ADB port forwarding, then open your private pairing link on the phone. Imported sources and recordings use the same desktop database.

adb -s YOUR_DEVICE_SERIAL reverse tcp:4317 tcp:4317

Use the host 127.0.0.1:4317 on your phone with your private pairing fragment. The laptop must be running and connected by USB. Native installation, offline synchronization, and background recording remain planned.
